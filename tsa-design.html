About the project

TSA Eclipse

TSA Culture Night is Carnegie Mellon University’s annual cross-cultural showcase hosted by the Taiwanese Student Association. An eclipse says: we each carry our own light, and when we align, the world stops to look. That’s the heart of TSA Culture Night. In this project, I created visual branding that’s powerful, bold, and adorned with a hint of mystery to celebrate cultures coming together to light up the moment during CMU’s highly anticipated Culture Night event.
